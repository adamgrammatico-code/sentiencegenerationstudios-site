Lazy Cat

A cozy one-tap arcade game. Tap to flap, glide between stacks of cat-tree posts and cushions, and see how far one very sleepy cat can float before it's nap time.

Every run brings new weather — rain, snow, drifting leaves or mist — and the cat reacts with a new expression for each action. Calm music, soft sounds, and sparkles wherever you tap.

Lazy Cat is free to play and supported by occasional ads. No account or sign-in is needed.
