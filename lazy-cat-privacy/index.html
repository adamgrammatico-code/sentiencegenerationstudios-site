Lazy Cat Privacy Policy

  • Developer: SentienceGeneration Studios
  • App: Lazy Cat (Android, package com.sentiencegen.lazycat)
  • Contact: adamgrammatico@sentiencegenerationstudios.com
  • Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the Lazy Cat game ("the app", "we", "us") handles and why. We wrote it to be short and plain.

1. The short version

  • Lazy Cat has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We (SentienceGeneration Studios) do not collect, store or sell any personal information on our own servers. We don't have any servers for this game.
  • Your best score and your music/sound settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). This is the only part of the app that collects data, as described below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To show ads, measure them and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

Data What it is Why
Device and account identifiers Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address Your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (approximate) location Advertising (e.g. serving ads for your region), fraud prevention
Product interactions Things like app launches, ad taps and video views Advertising, analytics
Diagnostic information App and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use Analytics, keeping ads working

All of this data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google processes it according to its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ads personalisation settings: https://adssettings.google.com

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app uses Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform, UMP) to ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can refuse, and you will still be able to play; you may then see non-personalised or limited ads. To change your choice later, clear the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Lazy Cat → Storage → Clear storage) and reopen the app, and the consent message will appear again.
  • Where US state privacy laws apply, Google's restricted data processing and the controls above apply.

We do not use the advertising ID for anything other than the Google ads described here, and we don't combine it with any other data.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location). The Google Mobile Ads SDK declares a few standard install-time permissions it needs to work: internet access, network state, the advertising ID permission (AD_ID), and the Android Privacy Sandbox ad-services permissions.

4. Children

Lazy Cat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Its intended audience is ages 13 and up. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and personal information was collected through the ads SDK, contact us at adamgrammatico@sentiencegenerationstudios.com and we will help, including asking Google to delete it where possible.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: the best score and settings stay until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Ads data: it is held by Google under Google's retention policies. Because we have no accounts and hold no personal data, there is nothing on our side to delete. You can still contact us with any question and we'll point you to the right Google controls.

6. Security

We don't operate servers or store your data. Data sent by the ads SDK travels over encrypted (TLS) connections.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests relate to Google's processing, and you can use Google's controls linked above. You can always contact us at adamgrammatico@sentiencegenerationstudios.com and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the "Effective date" above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
